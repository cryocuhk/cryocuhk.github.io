Lin Peng is a PhD student from the University of Exeter, jointly supported and mentored by Professors Stephan Harrison (Exeter), Karen Anderson (Exeter), and Lin Liu (CUHK). Her research focuses on the state and fate of rock glacier-associated waterbodies in High Mountain Asia. Her PhD is funded by the CUHK–University of Exeter Joint Centre for Environmental Sustainability & Resilience (ENSURE).
